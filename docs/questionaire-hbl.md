# Client Questionnaire: Hair by Lydia

## 1. Strategy: purpose and audience

**Q1.** In one sentence, what do you want the website to do for your business?  
**Lydia:** I want my website to help more people discover my business, improve my online visibility and make it easier for potential clients to find me and book appointments.

**Q2.** What would make you say in six months that the site has worked? (for example more enquiries, fewer messages going back and forth, better-quality clients)  
**Lydia:** I would know the website has worked if I start receiving more enquiries and gaining more bookings from new clients who have found me online.

**Q3.** Who are your best clients? Describe two or three real types of person, without names.  
**Lydia:** My ideal clients are women aged around 20–40 who love looking after their hair and want a high-quality, personalised service. They value beautiful colour, healthy-looking hair, convenience and building a relationship with a hairdresser they trust. They appreciate a luxury feel without losing the friendly, relaxed and personal experience of having their hair done in their own home.

**Q4.** Where do most of your clients find you now? (Instagram, word of mouth, Facebook, search)  
**Lydia:** Word of mouth, Instagram and Facebook.

**Q5.** What makes your service different from other hairdressers?  
**Lydia:** What makes my service different is that I offer a relaxed, personalised hairdressing experience in the comfort of your own home. I call myself ‘your personal hair fairy’ because I want to bring a little magic to my clients' hair whilst making their appointments feel easy, comfortable and stress-free. I'm really laid-back and friendly, and I want people to feel like they can be themselves around me, without the pressure or formality of a traditional salon.

I take pride in the little details, including leaving every home exactly as I found it. I take all my rubbish, used foils and washing away with me, so once I've left, it's almost like I was never there (apart from the lovely hair, of course!).

I also have extensive professional training from my time at Anne Veck, alongside experience in high-end salons and ongoing colour education. This means I can combine a friendly, down-to-earth approach with professional knowledge, technical skill and a real focus on achieving beautiful results whilst looking after the condition of my clients' hair.

I want my clients to feel like they've had a little bit of luxury and self-care brought to their doorstep, without having to leave the house. Whether they're busy parents, people who feel anxious in salon environments or simply someone who prefers their own space, I want every client to feel comfortable, looked after and confident in their hair.

**Q6.** Are there hairdressers or websites you admire, or ones you'd hate to look like? What do you like or dislike about them?  
**Lydia:** I like websites that look clean, modern and professional but still have personality. I'd want mine to feel feminine, a little whimsical and unique, without looking childish or overly girly. I want it to reflect my relaxed personality and the personal hair fairy branding, whilst still showing the quality of my work. I dislike websites that look too corporate, cluttered, overly complicated or generic. I'd like it to be easy to navigate, showcase my hair transformations beautifully and make it simple for clients to find my services, prices and contact details.

## 2. Scope: services and features

**Q7.** Is the 2026 price guide complete and final? Which prices might change, and how often?  
**Lydia:** My 2026 price guide is current, but prices may be reviewed occasionally to account for inflation and rising business costs. I try to keep my prices as consistent as possible for my clients, so I generally aim to make changes no more than once a year, if needed.

**Q8.** Which services need a consultation before booking?  
**Lydia:** I carry out an online consultation for every booking request to understand the client's hair history, goals and expectations before confirming the appointment. If a face-to-face consultation is needed or preferred by the client, we can arrange one before the appointment, particularly for more complex colour work or major hair transformations.

**Q9.** Do colour services need a patch test? How far ahead, and should the site mention it?  
**Lydia:** Patch tests can be arranged at my home address if required, particularly for clients who are new to hair colour or have not had their hair coloured before. I discuss colour history and any relevant concerns during my online consultation before confirming a booking. Patch testing requirements will be discussed where necessary, in line with the relevant product manufacturer's instructions and safety guidance. I do not need the site to mention this.

**Q10.** Do you want clients to book online, or send an enquiry that you reply to? Why?  
**Lydia:** I prefer clients to send an enquiry first, so I can discuss their hair goals, history and expectations before booking the appointment manually myself. This allows me to make sure we're booking the correct service, allowing enough time for the appointment and understanding exactly what the client wants before confirming their booking.

**Q11.** What do you currently use to manage bookings (phone, messages, diary, an app)?  
**Lydia:** I currently manage all my bookings using an online calendar where I keep track of appointments and client details. I also receive and manage booking enquiries through Facebook, Instagram and email, before manually adding confirmed appointments to my calendar.

**Q12.** What is your policy on deposits, cancellations and late arrivals, and should the site state it?  
**Lydia:** Yes, my cancellation policy should be clearly displayed on my website so clients understand the terms before booking. Deposits may also be required when booking an appointment.

My cancellation policy is:  
- 2 days' notice or more: No cancellation fee.  
- 1 day's notice: 50% of the booked service cost will be charged.  
- Cancellation on the day or failure to attend: 100% of the booked service cost will be charged.  
Any outstanding cancellation fees must be paid in full before future appointments can be booked or confirmed.

I understand that unexpected situations can happen, but as a self-employed hairdresser, late cancellations have a significant impact on my business. I want the policy to be communicated clearly but in a friendly, understanding way that still protects my time and income.

The website should also include information about deposits that may be required upon request.

**Q13.** Is there anything else you want on the site (about you, testimonials, FAQs, a blog, an Instagram feed)? Which matter most?  
**Lydia:** I would love a dedicated reviews section where clients can swipe through reviews or click an arrow to read the next one. I'd also like a link to my Instagram, ideally with a section showing my most recent posts so visitors can see my latest hair transformations and content.

I'd like an 'About Me' section to introduce myself, my experience and training, and explain the idea behind 'your personal hair fairy' so potential clients can get a feel for my personality before booking.

I'd also like a gallery showcasing my hair transformations, a clear price list, FAQs covering things like consultations, booking enquiries, deposits and cancellations, and an easy-to-use contact or enquiry form.

I'd love to include a 'Not sure what to book?' section to encourage clients to get in touch if they're unsure which service they need or how long their appointment will take. They could send an enquiry explaining their hair goals and current hair, allowing me to advise them on the best service before manually confirming their booking.

For the overall design, I'd like to keep the colours neutral, using warm browns, beige and creamy tones to create a clean, cosy and luxurious feel. I still want to incorporate subtle fairy-inspired details, particularly in the buttons and smaller design elements, to reflect my personal brand without making the website look too childish or overly girly.

I'd love the website to feel engaging and personal rather than just being a page of information. The main priorities are showcasing my work, building trust through reviews, helping people find out about my services and making it as easy as possible for new clients to enquire about an appointment.

## 3. Structure: travel and getting in touch

**Q14.** Which areas do you travel to? Does "within Banbury" mean the whole town or only central Banbury?  
**Lydia:** I cover central Banbury and the surrounding villages, with an additional travel fee based on the distance. I'd like the website to make this clear so clients know that travel charges may apply depending on their location.

**Q15.** How do travel charges work, and is there a minimum booking?  
**Lydia:** There is no minimum booking requirement. I cover central Banbury, with an additional travel charge for appointments further afield where taxi fares are approximately over £10. Any additional travel costs will be discussed with the client before their appointment is confirmed.

**Q16.** What are the best ways for clients to reach you (email, phone, Instagram, WhatsApp)? Which do you want the site to show?  
**Lydia:** I'd like clients to be able to contact me through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or email, as these are the platforms I currently use to communicate with clients and manage booking enquiries. I'd like the website to include easy-to-find links to all four so clients can contact me in whichever way they feel most comfortable.

**Q17.** What details do you need from someone before you can reply properly? How quickly do you usually reply?  
**Lydia:** I aim to respond to all enquiries as soon as possible, usually within one working day. Before I can advise a client properly, I'd need their name, phone number, email address and a brief description of what they're looking to have done or what they're considering.

It would also be really useful to include an option for clients to upload up to three photos with their enquiry. This could include photos of their current hair, their hair history or inspiration pictures of the look they're hoping to achieve. This would help me understand what they're looking for and give more accurate advice before discussing and confirming their appointment.

**Q18.** What is the first thing a visitor should see or be able to do when they open the site?  
**Lydia:** I'd like the homepage to immediately showcase my hairdressing work and introduce me as 'Your Personal Hair Fairy'. I want visitors to get a feel for my personality and my relaxed, personalised mobile hairdressing service straight away. The design should feel clean, cosy and luxurious, using neutral browns, beige and cream tones with subtle fairy-inspired details.

I'd like the main focus to be on my hair transformations, with clear buttons to 'Make an Enquiry', 'View My Work' and 'View Services & Prices'. It should also be clear that I cover central Banbury and surrounding villages. I want visitors to be able to find what they need easily and feel encouraged to enquire, even if they're not sure which service to book.

## 4. Surface: look and feel

**Q19.** Three words for how a client should feel when they look at the site  
**Lydia:** Welcomed, relaxed, inspired.

**Q20.** Do you have logo files, brand colours or fonts already, and are you happy with them?  
**Lydia:** I currently use two main fonts in Canva: Noto Serif Display Extra Condensed and Luxurious Script. I use Noto Serif Display Extra Condensed for the 'Hair by Lydia' text on my main branding photo, and Luxurious Script for other 'Hair by Lydia' text across my branding. I'm happy with both fonts and would like to keep them as part of my brand identity.

My brand colours are neutral and warm, with beige, cream and brown tones. I'd like to keep this colour palette for the website and build on it to create a clean, cosy and luxurious feel, with subtle fairy-inspired details to reflect my 'personal hair fairy' branding. I'm happy to explore how my existing branding could be brought together more consistently across the website.

**Q21.** Do you have photos of your work? Have clients agreed to them being published? Do you want before-and-afters?  
**Lydia:** I have a collection of photos of my hairdressing work, including before-and-after transformations and photos of the finished results. I always ask my clients for permission before posting their photos online, and they are happy for their hair to be shared. I'd like to use these photos throughout the website, particularly in a dedicated gallery, to showcase my work, highlight my colour transformations and help potential clients get an idea of the results I can achieve.

**Q22.** Are you happy for your credentials (years of experience, training, awards) to be shown? Do you have testimonials, and may they be used?  
**Lydia:** I'm happy for my professional experience, training and relevant achievements to be included on the website, as I think this helps build trust with potential clients. I'd like to highlight my training at Anne Veck, my experience working in high-end salons and my ongoing colour education.

I'd also like to include genuine client testimonials and reviews where permission has been given or where they can be shared appropriately. I want the website to show both my professional experience and the personal relationships I build with my clients.

## 5. Practical details and after launch

**Q23.** Do you have a website address, or do you want one?  
**Lydia:** I don't currently have a website or domain name, so I'd like to get one set up. Ideally, I'd like a simple, professional website address that's easy for clients to remember and recognisable as my brand, Hair by Lydia.

**Q24.** Who will update prices and photos after launch, and how often?  
**Lydia:** I would be happy to update my own prices and photos after the website launches, as I regularly take photos of my work and share them on Instagram. I'd like the website to be easy to manage so I can add new hair transformations whenever I have them and review my prices around once a year, if needed, to account for inflation and rising costs.

**Q25.** What is your deadline, if any? How much time can you give to reviewing the site and sending content?  
**Lydia:** I don't have a strict deadline at the moment, but I'd like to get the website up and running as soon as reasonably possible. I can make time to review the website, provide photos and content, and give feedback throughout the process. I'd prefer to work through it in manageable stages so I can make sure everything reflects my business and branding properly before it goes live.

**Q26.** Is there anything that worries you about having a website?  
**Lydia:** My main concern is making sure the website is easy to manage and keep up to date without it becoming too time-consuming or complicated. I'd also want it to reflect my personality and branding properly, attract the right clients and help generate more enquiries and bookings. I'd like to understand any ongoing costs involved, such as the domain name, hosting and maintenance, so there are no unexpected expenses.
